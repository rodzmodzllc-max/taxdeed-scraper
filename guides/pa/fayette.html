How to buy tax-sale property in Fayette County, PA

What the Fayette County offices publish about buying tax-sale property: who runs each sale or list, when and where, and what a buyer must do. Every answer below was read on an official page and links to it. Questions not yet confirmed are listed as such.

Available

Repository property: parcels unsold at the tax sales, offered by the Tax Claim Bureau by bid.

Which office handles it and how do I contact them?

Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau, 2 West Main Street, Uniontown PA 15401; phone 724-430-1208; Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.

“Tax Claim Bureau 2 West Main Street Uniontown PA 15401 Directions Phone 724-430-1208 Fax 724-491-2928 Hours Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.”

Source: Tax Sale Information | Fayette County, PA · verified 2026-11-02

What is this list and why is the property available? Statewide

Property that remains unsold after being exposed to a judicial sale is placed in a "repository for unsold properties". The county tax claim bureau keeps the list and makes it available to the public during normal office hours.

“If, after conducting and exposing a property to a judicial sale under the provisions of this act, whether prior to, at the time of or after the effective date of this amendatory act, any property remains unsold, it shall be placed in a category to be termed "repository for unsold properties." A list of properties in this category shall be maintained by the bureau and shall be available during normal office hours to the general public.”

Source: Real Estate Tax Sale Law, Act of July 7, 1947, P.L. 1368, No. 542 (Pennsylvania General Assembly) · Real Estate Tax Sale Law sec. 626 (72 P.S. 5860.626) · verified 2026-11-02

How do I buy a property from this list?

Repository properties can be bid on at any time by sealed bid. Bids are opened on the first Tuesday of the month at 10:00 a.m.; the municipality and school district accept or reject the bid, and the bureau notifies by mail within 30-45 days.

“If the property still does not sell, it is put on our Repository List and can be bid on at any time. We keep the sealed bids in a lockbox. We open the envelopes on the first Tuesday of the month at 10:00 a.m. It is the decision of the authorities to accept or reject your bid. (Municipality and School District) We will notify you by mail within 30-45 days of the decision.”

Source: Tax Sale Information | Fayette County, PA · verified 2026-11-02

How is the price set? Statewide

For repository property, the bureau may set a minimum purchase price with the written consent of all taxing districts and accept an offer at or above it.

“establish a minimum purchase price and accept an offer of any price equal to or greater than the minimum purchase price”

Source: Real Estate Tax Sale Law, Act of July 7, 1947, P.L. 1368, No. 542 (Pennsylvania General Assembly) · Real Estate Tax Sale Law sec. 627(a) (72 P.S. 5860.627) · verified 2026-11-02

What costs are added to the price? Statewide

The bureau records the deed at the purchaser's expense. A county that has adopted the ordinance may also collect a fee of up to $250 from the buyer at the time of sale for its demolition and rehabilitation fund.

“It shall be the responsibility of the bureau to have the deed recorded at the expense of the purchaser. ... The governing body of a county may, by ordinance, impose a fee not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars ($250) for the sale of any property being sold in accordance with this act”

Source: Real Estate Tax Sale Law, Act of July 7, 1947, P.L. 1368, No. 542 (Pennsylvania General Assembly) · Real Estate Tax Sale Law sec. 627(b), 631(b) · verified 2026-11-02

What forms or documents are required?

The bureau's checklist: one bid form, the Affidavit of Bidder(s), a money order, certified check or cashier's check for the bid amount, in an envelope marked "Sealed Repository Bid".

“CHECK LIST: ... ONE BID FORM ... AFFIDAVIT OF BIDDER(S) ... MONEY ORDER, CERTIFIED CHECK, OR CASHIER'S CHECK FOR BID AMOUNT ... ENVELOPE MARKED SEALED REPOSITORY BID”

Source: Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau - Repository Sale Sealed Bid Form · verified 2026-11-02

How and when do I pay?

Enclose a money order, certified check or cashier's check for the bid amount with the sealed bid.

“MONEY ORDER, CERTIFIED ... CHECK, OR CASHIER'S CHECK ... FOR BID AMOUNT”

Source: Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau - Repository Sale Sealed Bid Form · verified 2026-11-02

What kind of deed or title does the buyer receive? Statewide

Repository property is conveyed free and clear of all tax and municipal claims, mortgages, liens, charges and estates of any kind, except ground rents separately taxed.

“The property shall be conveyed to the purchaser upon payment of the agreed price, free and clear of all tax and municipal claims, mortgages, liens, and charges and estates of whatsoever kind, except ground rents separately taxed.”

Source: Real Estate Tax Sale Law, Act of July 7, 1947, P.L. 1368, No. 542 (Pennsylvania General Assembly) · Real Estate Tax Sale Law sec. 627(b) · verified 2026-11-02

How long does a property stay available?

Property that does not sell goes on the Repository List and can be bid on at any time. Sealed bids are opened on the first Tuesday of the month, bidders are notified by mail within 30-45 days of the decision, and recording the deed may take up to six months.

“If the property still does not sell, it is put on our Repository List and can be bid on at any time. ... We open the envelopes on the first Tuesday of the month at 10:00 a.m. ... We will notify you by mail within 30-45 days of the decision. Deeds are typed in our office; it may take up to six months to have them recorded.”

Source: Fayette County, PA - Tax Claim Bureau · Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau · verified 2026-11-02

Which liens or interests stay with the property? Statewide

Repository property is conveyed on payment of the agreed price free and clear of all tax and municipal claims, mortgages, liens, charges and estates, except ground rents separately taxed. The deed is recorded at the purchaser's expense.

“The property shall be conveyed to the purchaser upon payment of the agreed price, free and clear of all tax and municipal claims, mortgages, liens, and charges and estates of whatsoever kind, except ground rents separately taxed. It shall be the responsibility of the bureau to have the deed recorded at the expense of the purchaser.”

Source: Real Estate Tax Sale Law, Act of July 7, 1947, P.L. 1368, No. 542 (Pennsylvania General Assembly) · 72 P.S. 5860.627(b) · verified 2026-11-02

Is the title insurable or does it need a quiet-title action?

Properties are offered without any guarantee or warranty as to the record title or otherwise, and no adjustments are made after the bureau accepts a bid.

“ALL PROPERTIES ARE OFFERED FOR SALE WITHOUT ANY GUARANTEE OR WARRANTY WHATSOEVER, AS TO THE RECORD TITLE OR OTHERWISE, AND NO ADJUSTMENTS OF ANY KIND WILL BE MADE AFTER THE TAX CLAIM BUREAU HAS ACCEPTED YOUR BID.”

Source: Tax Claim Bureau - Affidavit of Repository Bidder (Fayette County, PA) · Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau · verified 2026-11-02

Can I inspect the property before buying?

The bureau's affidavit tells bidders to conduct their own inspection of the title before placing a bid, because the bureau does not guarantee that the property is not slated for demolition or condemnation; it does not address physical inspection of the property.

“ALSO BE ADVISED THAT THE TAX CLAIM BUREAU DOES NOT GUARANTEE THAT THIS PROPERTY HAS NOT BEEN SLATED FOR DEMOLITION OR CONDEMNATION. FOR THAT REASON, BE ADVISED THAT IT WOULD BE PRUDENT TO CONDUCT YOUR OWN INSPECTION OF THE TITLE TO THE PROPERTY YOU ARE PURCHACING PRIOR TO PLACING YOUR BID.”

Source: Tax Claim Bureau - Affidavit of Repository Bidder (Fayette County, PA) · Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau · verified 2026-11-02

Are sales final or can a purchase be refunded?

All sales are final and there are no refunds; a bidder found delinquent in taxes or municipal utility bills in Fayette County may not receive a deed and forfeits the bid price.

“SHOULD WE DISCOVER THAT YOU ARE DELINQUENT IN TAXES OR MUNICIPAL UTILITY BILLS ON ANY PROPERTY IN FAYETTE COUNTY, YOU MAY NOT RECEIVE A DEED TO THE PROPERTY ON WHICH YOU BID AND YOU WILL FORFEIT YOUR BID PRICE. I UNDERSTAND AND ACKNOWLEDGE THAT ALL SALES ARE FINAL. THERE ARE NO REFUNDS.”

Source: Tax Claim Bureau - Affidavit of Repository Bidder (Fayette County, PA) · Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau · verified 2026-11-02

What does the law or the program tell buyers to check before buying?

The Tax Claim Bureau strongly suggests consulting an attorney before purchasing at any of its sales and states it offers no warranty or guarantee at any sale it conducts.

“We, The Tax Claim Bureau offer no warranty or guarantee whatsoever at any sale we conduct. ... It is strongly suggested that you consult with an attorney if you purchase any properties at our sales.”

Source: Fayette County, PA - Tax Claim Bureau · Fayette County Tax Claim Bureau · verified 2026-11-02
