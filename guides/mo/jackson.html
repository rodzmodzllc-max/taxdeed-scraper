What is this list and why is the property available? Statewide

A municipality in a county operating wholly under the Land Tax Collection Law may establish a land bank agency to manage, sell, transfer and dispose of real estate the agency owns.

Official wording
“Any municipality located wholly or partially within a county electing to operate wholly under the provisions of sections 141.210 to 141.810 may establish a land bank agency for the management, sale, transfer, and other disposition of interests in real estate owned by such land bank agency.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 141.980 RSMo · RSMo 141.980 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Step 3 · Buy How to buy

How do I buy a property from this list?

On the public property site, a property that is available for sale shows an Apply for this Property button; the buyer creates a login account for the Public Site, completes the application and submits it.

Official wording
“If a property is available for sale, a button will display labeled ... Apply for this Property. ... Click the button. ... If you do not yet have a login account for the Public Site, please create one.”

Source: Kansas City Land Bank - ePropertyPlus public site settings · Kansas City Municipal Land Bank · Checked Nov 2, 2026

What forms or documents are required?

The Land Bank's property portal lists separate purchase applications, including one for side lots (for an owner who lives in an abutting property) and one for individuals buying a property that is not a side lot or an urban agriculture project.

Official wording
“Property Purchase Application - Side Lots (Individuals) ... Use this application if you own and reside in a property abutting a Land Bank or KCMHA property and want to purchase it to expand the existing property. ... Property Purchase Application - Individuals ... Use this application if you are an individual and want to purchase a property that does not qualify as a Side Lot or an Urban Agriculture project.”

Source: Kansas City Land Bank - ePropertyPlus public site settings · Kansas City Municipal Land Bank · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Auctions 12 of 17 answered

Step 1 · Find Find the sale or list

How does a tax sale work here? Statewide

Land on which taxes or special assessments are delinquent is subject to sale to discharge the tax lien, held on the fourth Monday in August each year.

Official wording
“All lands, lots, mineral rights, and royalty interests on which taxes or special assessments are delinquent and unpaid are subject to sale to discharge the lien for the delinquent and unpaid taxes or unpaid special assessments as provided for in this chapter on the fourth Monday in August of each year.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.150 RSMo · RSMo 140.150 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

When are sales held? Statewide

The sale is held in or next to the courthouse on the fourth Monday in August, starting at ten o'clock and continuing from day to day until all land is offered.

Official wording
“will be sold at public auction to discharge the taxes, penalty, interest, and costs due thereon at the time of sale in or adjacent to the courthouse of such county, on the fourth Monday in August next thereafter, commencing at ten o'clock of said day and continuing from day to day thereafter until all are offered.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.170 RSMo · RSMo 140.170 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Where do I bid - online or in person? Statewide

The auction is held in or next to the county courthouse; the collector may also run it at the same time by electronic media, including the internet.

Official wording
“in or adjacent to the courthouse of such county ... Such auction may also be conducted by electronic media, including the internet, at the same time and at the discretion of the county collector.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.170 RSMo · RSMo 140.170 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

What makes up the opening or minimum bid? Statewide

At the first offering, if no one bids a sum equal to the delinquent taxes with interest, penalty and costs, the land is not sold and is offered again at the next sale.

Official wording
“If at the first offering of sale of any tract of land or lot under the provisions of this law, no person bids therefor a sum equal to the delinquent taxes thereon with interest, penalty and costs”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.240 RSMo · RSMo 140.240 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

What does the law or the county tell buyers to check before buying? Statewide

The purchaser must obtain a title search report from a licensed attorney or licensed title company detailing the ownership and encumbrances on the property.

Official wording
“The purchaser shall obtain a title search report from a licensed attorney or licensed title company detailing the ownership and encumbrances on the property.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.405 RSMo · RSMo 140.405 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Step 3 · Buy How to buy

How do I register to bid? Statewide

No sale is made to a person who is currently delinquent on taxes on other property and does not sign an affidavit stating such at the time of sale. A nonresident must file a written consent to the jurisdiction of the county's circuit court.

Official wording
“no sale shall be made to any person or designated agent who: (a) Is currently delinquent on any tax payments on any property, other than a delinquency on the property being offered for sale, and who does not sign an affidavit stating such at the time of sale. ... A nonresident shall file with said collector an agreement in writing consenting to the jurisdiction of the circuit court of the county in which such sale shall be made”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.190 RSMo · RSMo 140.190 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

When and how must the winning bidder pay? Statewide

The purchaser must pay the bid to the collector immediately. A purchaser who fails to pay forfeits a penalty of twenty-five percent of the bid, and the land is offered again.

Official wording
“the purchaser at such sale shall immediately pay the amount of his bid to the collector ... In case the purchaser fails to pay his bid, the land shall be again forthwith offered for sale the same as if no sale had been made, and the purchaser so failing shall forfeit and pay for the use of the distributive county school fund of the county a penalty of twenty-five percent of the amount of his bid”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.280 RSMo · RSMo 140.280 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

What costs are added on top of the winning bid? Statewide

For each certificate of purchase, the purchaser pays the collector the fee to record the certificate with the county recorder, as part of the cost of the sale.

Official wording
“For each certificate of purchase issued, as a part of the cost of the sale, the purchaser shall pay to the collector the fee necessary to record such certificate of purchase in the office of the county recorder.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.290 RSMo · RSMo 140.290 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Step 4 · After After you buy

What kind of deed or title does the buyer receive? Statewide

The collector executes a deed in the name of the state that vests an absolute estate in fee simple, subject to claims for unpaid taxes (other than certain inferior ones).

Official wording
“The collector of the county in which the sale of such lands took place shall execute to the purchaser or the purchaser's heirs or assigns, in the name of the state, a conveyance of the real estate so sold, which shall vest in the grantee an absolute estate in fee simple, subject, however, to all claims thereon for unpaid taxes”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.420 RSMo · RSMo 140.420 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Can the former owner redeem after the sale? Statewide

The owner, lienholder, occupant or others with an interest have an absolute right to redeem for one year after the sale, and a defeasible right until the purchaser acquires the deed.

Official wording
“shall have the absolute right to redeem the same at any time during the one year next ensuing and shall continue to have a defeasible right to redeem the same until such time as the tax sale purchaser acquires the deed”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.340 RSMo · RSMo 140.340 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

What happens to property that does not sell? Statewide

If land is not sold at the third offering, the collector advertises or offers it for sale once every thirty days.

Official wording
“If any lands or lots are not sold at such third offering, then the collector shall advertise or offer such lands or lots for sale once every thirty days.”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.250 RSMo · RSMo 140.250 · Checked Nov 2, 2026

Step 5 · Risks Risks to weigh

Which liens or interests survive the sale? Statewide

The deed is subject to claims for unpaid taxes, except unpaid taxes whose lien was inferior to the lien for which the land was sold.

Official wording
“subject, however, to all claims thereon for unpaid taxes except such unpaid taxes, existing at time of the purchase of said lands and the lien for which taxes was inferior to the lien for taxes for which said tract or lot of land was sold”

Source: Missouri Revisor of Statutes - Section 140.420 RSMo · RSMo 140.420 · Checked Nov 2, 2026
