How to buy tax-sale property in Green County, WI

What the Green County offices publish about buying tax-sale property: who runs each sale or list, when and where, and what a buyer must do. Every answer below was read on an official page and links to it. Questions not yet confirmed are listed as such.

Auctions

A county sale of tax-deeded land by bid on the county's form.

Which office handles it and how do I contact them?

The Green County Clerk's Office, 1016 16th Ave, Monroe, WI 53566, phone (608) 328-9430, handles the tax deed sales.

“County Clerk's Office, 1016 16th Ave, Monroe, WI 53566. ... Please contact the County Clerk's office at (608) 328-9430 with questions regarding the sale of this property.”

Source: Green County Tax Deed Real Estate Bid Sheet · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

Where do I bid - online or in person?

All tax deed sales are held at the Green County Historic Courthouse, 1016 16th Ave, at the date and time listed for each sale.

“All tax deed sales will be held at the Green County Historic Courthouse located at 1016 16th Ave at the date and time specified below.”

Source: Current Tax Deed Sales | Green County, WI · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

How do I register to bid?

Bidders complete a bid form and return it to the Green County Clerk's Office in a sealed envelope labeled with the site address or parcel number.

“Persons interested in bidding on tax deeded property shall complete a bid form and return to the Green County Clerk's Office in a sealed envelope labeled with the site address/parcel number”

Source: Current Tax Deed Sales | Green County, WI · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

When and how must the winning bidder pay?

Full payment is due within 10 business days of the bid being awarded, and the County Clerk contacts the buyer after the sale to set a closing date.

“Full payment is due within 10 business days of bid being awarded. The County Clerk will contact you after the sale to set up a closing date.”

Source: Current Tax Deed Sales | Green County, WI · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

What costs are added on top of the winning bid?

Successful bidders are responsible for any future special assessment installments and property taxes.

“Successful bidders will be responsible for any future special assessment installments and property taxes.”

Source: Green County Tax Deed Real Estate Bid Sheet · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

Is the title insurable or does it need a quiet-title action?

Tax deed property is sold as is, where is and without conditions, and determining any defects in the title or the property is the responsibility of prospective purchasers.

“All tax deed property will be sold "AS IS, WHERE IS and WITHOUT CONDITIONS." ... It is the responsibility of all prospective purchasers to determine any defects in the title or the property.”

Source: Green County Tax Deed Real Estate Bid Sheet · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02

What does the law or the county tell buyers to check before buying?

The county makes no representations or warranties about the ability to build, zoning, or environmental conditions such as toxins, contaminants, radon, hazardous substances or storage tanks, and reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

“Green County makes no representations, assurances or warranties as to, without limitation due to enumeration, the ability to build on the land, zoning and environmental condition such as the presence of toxins, contaminants, radon, hazardous substances or storage tanks, of and on the properties listed for sale. ... Green County reserves the right to reject any or all bids.”

Source: Green County Tax Deed Real Estate Bid Sheet · Green County Clerk · verified 2026-11-02
