How to read this data

This app collects what county and vendor sale sites publish, on a schedule, and shows it as collected. It is a research tool. It is not a title search, not an appraisal, and not a record of what happened at a sale.

Where each row comes from

Every property page names its Data source (the county auction site, the county's Lands Available list, LienHub, a Texas vendor listing) and its last synced time. County value figures come from the tax roll for the stated year. Coordinates come from a geocoder, flood zones from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, imagery from USDA aerial photography - each labelled with its source where it appears.

What the missing-data words mean

Not published - the source did not post that figure (an opening bid, a parcel number). It does not mean zero or free.

Not checked - this app has not looked it up yet (a flood zone, an image). It does not mean there is nothing to find.

Not tracked - no source in this app carries that information at all (liens, judgments, code violations, sale outcomes). Absence here is never evidence of absence.

Not mapped by FEMA - FEMA publishes no flood map for that spot. It is not the same as low risk.

The source of sale is authoritative

The county's own auction site, the Clerk, the Property Appraiser and the Tax Collector are the record. When this app and the county disagree, the county is right and this app is behind. Sale dates move, listings are withdrawn, bids are re-posted; the Data sources panel on the Dashboard shows when each dataset last synced and whether the last run was complete.

Sale event history

A property page lists each scheduled sale date this app observed for it and what the source showed each time. "Sale date passed - outcome not tracked" means the listing left the source's feed after its date. It does not mean the property sold, was redeemed, or was cancelled - this app records no sale results, winning bids or purchasers, and never infers one from a listing disappearing. "Superseded" means the source later listed the same property under a new date.

Ratios and estimates

Value ÷ bid is the county value on file divided by the opening bid - arithmetic for screening, not a return or a margin. Fee figures are estimates with a stated open question. Nothing here is a recommendation to bid.

Before you bid

Verify every figure on the county's own site, order a title search, and have a title company or attorney review the parcel. Liens, code enforcement actions and other encumbrances survive a tax deed sale and are not visible here.

Terms & disclaimer

This is a research tool, not a title search. Everything here is a best-effort reading of public records. It is not a certified title search, a title opinion, an appraisal, or legal or financial advice.

Before you bid

Confirm every figure against the county's own auction site, the Clerk of Court, the Property Appraiser and the Tax Collector, and have a title company or attorney review the parcel. A tax deed can carry liens, code enforcement actions, and other encumbrances this tool does not and cannot see.

Where the numbers come from

Property data is collected from county and vendor websites. Texas collection is currently run by hand, not on a fixed schedule, so a Texas listing can be days or weeks behind the source. Data can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date — a county or vendor can change or withdraw a listing at any time, including after our last sync.

A property whose own row has not been re-synced recently carries an amber edge in the list, so a stale record is visible where you are actually reading, not only in aggregate here.

Opening bid / minimum bid is the figure the vendor or county published. It is not the cost to acquire. "Not published" means no figure was posted, not that the property is free.

Value, where shown, is the figure the vendor listing carries. It is not an appraisal or a comparative market analysis. No Texas appraisal-district values are loaded.

Fees and closing costs are not estimated for Texas. This app has no Texas fee formula and shows none. Texas redemption rights, deed type and post-sale costs are not tracked here.

Lien notes are not tracked for Texas listings. This app does not run a title search or give a title opinion.
